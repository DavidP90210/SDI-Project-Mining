Laboratory Scale Flotation System for Minerals

An accessible, controllable flotation system for small-scale laboratory use, combining processing stages into one platform for repeatable testing.

Value Proposition

Lab flotation usually means separate steps for preparation, grinding, flotation, and collection. Our system brings them together with more control and less complexity.

Gain Creators

  • Integrated processing stages
  • Repeatable results
  • Better experimental control

Pain Relievers

  • Simplified workflow
  • Accessible lab operation
  • Easier sample handling

Balanced Operation

A balance between manual operation and automation, with lower system complexity while maintaining experimental control.

Design Specifications

Key physical and operating requirements for the laboratory-scale flotation cell.

Cell Geometry

Bench-scale design with a near 1:1 aspect ratio, cylindrical or cubic.

Agitation

Bottom-mounted impeller with variable motor-speed control.

Air Injection

Forced-air system with a compressor, sealed tubing, and an adjustable flow valve.

Instrumentation

Pressure transducers, thermocouples, and flowmeters for slurry and air monitoring.

Slurry Handling

Spill tray and accessible drain for cleaner operation and maintenance.

Accessibility

Layout designed for easy adjustment, observation, and laboratory use.

Final Concept

Our CAD concept is a cylindrical cell with a funnel-shaped top, a side outlet for froth, and a bottom port. Rough calculations and technical analysis will be added here as the design develops.

Key Takeaways

  • Airflow creates and distributes bubbles
  • Both variables should be adjustable
